Juggle math

A siteswap pattern copied off a forum can call for two balls on the same beat, a fractional ball, or a throw that only a ceiling fan survives. Juggle math checks the pattern before the practice: the average names the ball count, the landing beats name the collisions, the throw and a labeled beat name the height, and the beat names the tempo. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Pattern plan - a siteswap pattern becomes the ball count, or an honest reason it fails: a fractional ball or two throws sharing a landing beat.
  • Throw plan - a throw value at a labeled beat becomes the air time and the apex height.
  • Tempo plan - a labeled beat becomes the throws a minute, across the hands and per hand.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 400 ms beat, two hands, gravity 9.81 m/s2, throws as single siteswap digits 1 to 9 - all illustrative defaults you can change. Real juggling depends on dwell time, body throws and the ceiling you actually have. This is planning arithmetic, not a coaching verdict: learn the pattern on grass, over a soft landing.
